## Overview
GoBuyMe is a comprehensive on-demand delivery platform that connects users with local restaurants and errand services, offering a seamless experience from ordering to doorstep delivery.

## Getting Started

### Account Creation
1. Download and install the GoBuyMe app
2. Sign up using your email or social media accounts
3. Complete your profile information
4. Add your delivery address and payment method

### First-Time User Experience
- Take a quick tour of the app
- Enable location services for better recommendations
- Explore nearby restaurants and services

## Core Features

### 1. Food Ordering
- Browse restaurants and menus
- Customize your order
- Apply promo codes
- Schedule orders

### 2. Real-time Tracking
- Track your order status
- Live delivery agent location
- Estimated delivery time

### 3. Referral Program
- Share your referral code
- Earn credits on successful referrals
- Track your earnings

### 4. Order Management
- View order history
- Reorder with one tap
- Rate and review orders

## User Guide

### Placing an Order
1. Open the app and sign in
2. Browse restaurants or search for specific cuisines
3. Select items and add to cart
4. Review your order and apply any promo codes
5. Select delivery address and payment method
6. Confirm and place order

### Managing Your Account
- Update profile information
- Save multiple delivery addresses
- Add/remove payment methods
- Set delivery preferences

### Troubleshooting

#### Common Issues
- **Login Problems**: Reset password or verify email
- **Order Not Received**: Check order status or contact support
- **Payment Issues**: Verify payment method or try another card

### FAQ

**Q: How do I track my order?**
A: Go to 'My Orders' and select your active order to see real-time tracking.

**Q: How do I apply a promo code?**
A: Add items to cart > Proceed to checkout > Enter promo code in the designated field.

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**
A: We accept all major credit/debit cards, mobile money, and wallet payments.
